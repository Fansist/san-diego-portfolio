Our Story

A Neighborhood Shop, Not A Chain

Genesee Plaza Dry Cleaners is a well-established dry cleaning and alterations shop tucked into Genesee Plaza near the Clairemont and Kearny Mesa border, offering same-day dry cleaning, alterations, and wedding dress cleaning and preservation. Long-time customers point to the same familiar staff for careful alterations and dress restoration work, with some loyal patrons driving across town after five-plus years of relying on the shop. It has been named a Nextdoor Neighborhood Favorite twice, in 2017 and 2019, for exactly that kind of dependability.

Built on 102 real customer reviews and a 4.1 rating, it has earned a reputation as a dependable, unpretentious neighborhood cleaner in Genesee Plaza.
