Our Story

A Trusted Fixture On Adams Avenue

Charles Alteration & Tailoring has been a neighborhood tailoring institution on Adams Avenue for over 20 years, handling everything from simple hems and zipper repairs to full custom tailoring for men, women, and kids. The shop also offers 3-day dry cleaning, laundry, and pressing. Regulars describe the staff as friendly and meticulous, measuring and adjusting each garment on the spot to get the fit exactly right before it leaves the shop.

With a 4.0 rating built on 120-plus reviews, it has earned a reputation as a trustworthy, reasonably priced neighborhood gem in the Adams Avenue business district.
