How It Works

The Rooted process

Every call follows the same four-part path, from first phone call to a yard that stays healthy long after the visit.

  1. 1

    Call it in

    Describe what's going on, a dry patch, a leaking valve, a yard that needs work, and get a fast response.

  2. 2

    Diagnose the system

    Sprinkler heads, drip lines, timers, and zones get checked so the actual problem gets found, not guessed at.

  3. 3

    Fix, install, or maintain

    Leak repair, new irrigation installs, timer programming, or routine lawn care, handled in one visit when possible.

  4. 4

    The yard stays rooted

    Healthy, evenly watered, and looking cared for, with a team you can call again when it needs attention.
