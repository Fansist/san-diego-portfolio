Our story

From sermon to sonnet.

Trade credit, instantly

Estimate your books before you drive down

Type in an ISBN, pick a genre and condition, and we'll give you a ballpark credit value. Final numbers are set at the counter, but this should tell you whether it's worth the trip.

How trade credit works

  • We pay more for rare, first-edition, and local-author titles.
  • Credit is good toward any purchase — books, journals, readings.
  • Bring boxes, bags, or single volumes — walk-ins welcome.
  • Estate lots and library dispersals by appointment.

Current want list: modern first editions, San Diego poetry chapbooks, philosophy of mind, leather-bound sets pre-1920.
