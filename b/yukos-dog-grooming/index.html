How every visit goes

The calm cut ritual

Gentle handling and Japanese-style precision cuts are not separate things here, they are the same unhurried approach applied from the moment your dog walks in to the last snip.

01

Settle in

Every dog gets a calm, unhurried welcome first. The pace stays slow on purpose, which is what puts nervous dogs at ease instead of rushing them through the door.

02

Bath and brush

A soothing wash and a thorough brush-out that works out tangles gently, leaving the coat clean and ready for shaping.

03

Precision finish

Careful scissor work with a Japanese-style eye for detail, a neat, breed-appropriate cut instead of a quick one-size-fits-all clip.

Our story

Calm hands, careful cuts

Yuko's Dog Grooming is a long-running, single-location salon on Euclid Avenue known for gentle handling and Japanese-style precision cuts. It's the kind of neighborhood groomer where anxious dogs settle down and regulars keep coming back, personal and unhurried rather than corporate and rushed. From a full service groom to a quick nail trim, every visit gets the same calm, careful approach.
