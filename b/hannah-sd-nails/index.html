Hannah SD Nails is a luxury, appointment-only nail studio in North Park founded by a celebrity nail artist who has worked with Janet Jackson and Kali Uchis. The studio is especially known for custom anime and 3D nail art and hand-painted designs, alongside Gel-X extensions, structured gel manicures, and Japanese manicures and Dazzle Dry for clients focused on natural-nail health and clean beauty.
