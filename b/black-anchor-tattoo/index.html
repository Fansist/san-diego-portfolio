Heal it right

AFTERCARE
TIMELINE

free kit with every piece
Day 1–3

Keep it covered

Do

  • · Leave the second skin (Saniderm) on for 24–72 hrs as directed
  • · Wash gently with unscented soap & warm water 2x daily
  • · Pat dry with clean paper towels

Don't

  • · Don't pick at the bandage or scabs
  • · No soaking, swimming, or long showers
  • · Avoid tight clothing rubbing the area
Day 4–7

Light peeling begins

Do

  • · Switch to a thin layer of unscented lotion 2x daily
  • · Let flakes fall off naturally — they will
  • · Keep it clean and dry between applications

Don't

  • · Don't scratch, pick, or peel flakes
  • · No exfoliants, acids, or retinoids near the area
  • · Skip the gym if it causes heavy sweat on the area
Week 2

Itching peaks

Do

  • · Continue light moisturizing — a little goes a long way
  • · Slap (don't scratch) if it itches
  • · Wear loose, breathable fabrics over it

Don't

  • · Don't expose to direct sun
  • · No tanning beds, period
  • · No sauna, hot tub, or ocean until fully closed
Week 3–4

Surface healed

Do

  • · Resume normal activity
  • · Start daily SPF 30+ once skin is fully closed
  • · Keep moisturized to support deep-layer settling

Don't

  • · Don't skip sunscreen — UV fades ink fast
  • · No re-tattooing over the area for 6 weeks
  • · Don't panic if it looks slightly dull — it'll brighten
Month 2+

Fully settled

Do

  • · Schedule a free touch-up consult if any spots need it
  • · Photograph in natural light for our touch-up reference
  • · Keep up SPF daily for life

Don't

  • · Don't wait more than 6 months for a touch-up consult
  • · No heavy laser treatments nearby without telling us first
  • · Don't judge the final color until 60 days post-session
