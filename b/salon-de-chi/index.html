The Signature Specialty

How Straightening Works

Japanese hair straightening is a longer, more deliberate appointment. Here is what the process involves.

1

Consultation

Chi reviews your hair type, texture, and goals before any heat or treatment begins.

2

Relaxing Treatment

A smoothing solution is applied strand by strand to ease the curl pattern.

3

Thermal Pressing

A flat iron sets the hair smooth, section by section, with a careful hand.

4

Neutralize & Finish

A finishing treatment locks in the result and the style is finished for you to leave with.

The Studio

Chi's Craft, Since 1996

Salon de Chi is a boutique one-stylist salon inside Phenix Salon Suites, led by Chi, whose styling career began in Japan in 1996. The studio was established in San Diego in 2016 and specializes in Japanese hair straightening, precision cuts with the Toni and Guy scissor method, and color tailored to curly, fine, and thick hair. Every appointment is one-on-one, from consultation to final strand.
