From the dock to your kitchen

Howtocookit

Four methods, the fish that loves each, and the way we’d cook it ourselves. Call us anytime if you forget the timing.

Pan-Sear

Yellowtail · White Sea Bass · Halibut

  1. 1.Pat fish dry, season with salt 10 min before.
  2. 2.Cast-iron or stainless pan, oil shimmering hot.
  3. 3.Skin-side down 3–4 min, press flat for first 30 sec.
  4. 4.Flip, kill the heat, baste with butter 60 sec.

Grill

Halibut · Swordfish · Albacore steaks

  1. 1.Oil the grate, not the fish — medium-high heat.
  2. 2.Skin-on cuts only; crosshatch 3 min per side.
  3. 3.Brush with herb butter in last 60 seconds.
  4. 4.Pull at 130°F internal — carryover finishes it.

Ceviche

Rock Cod · Halibut · Spot Prawns

  1. 1.Cube fish 1/4 inch, chill bowl first.
  2. 2.Cover with fresh lime juice, fridge 15–20 min.
  3. 3.Fold in red onion, chili, cilantro, salt.
  4. 4.Eat within 2 hours — it keeps cooking.

Smoke

Albacore · Salmon · Yellowtail belly

  1. 1.Brine 4 hours in 4% salt + brown sugar.
  2. 2.Pat dry, air-dry 1 hr until pellicle forms.
  3. 3.Alder or apple wood, 180°F for 2–3 hours.
  4. 4.Rest 30 min before slicing against the grain.
