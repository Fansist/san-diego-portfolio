A downtown studio built on personal touch

My Friend's Flower Shop is a downtown San Diego floral studio known for custom-designed arrangements spanning traditional, contemporary, and dramatic over-the-top styles. The team hand-selects every stem from trusted vendors for freshness and quality, and is known among regulars for going the extra mile, including meeting clients on days the studio is normally closed. It is a longtime favorite for thoughtful Passion Florals gifting and special-occasion pieces in the heart of the city.
