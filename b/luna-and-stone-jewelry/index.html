Bespoke journey

Custom design, start to finish

Four steps from first conversation to final polish — every milestone approved by you, every piece one of a kind.

01

Consultation

A 60-minute bench session. We talk about the piece, your story, and the stones you love. You leave with a hand-drawn sketch and a quote.

02

Sketch & render

Two weeks of refining — hand-drawn sketches become a 3D render. We adjust proportion, stone, and metal until you say yes.

03

Wax carve

We carve a wax model so you can try on the actual proportion, weight, and feel before any gold is cast.

04

Cast, set, finish

Lost-wax cast in recycled gold, stones hand-set, polished, hallmarked, and delivered in a linen pouch.
