Our story

A van, a driveway, a calmer groom

Pawsitive Friends is a mobile dog grooming van serving greater San Diego, bringing full grooms, baths, and nail trims straight to the driveway. The fully equipped van means no car ride and no waiting room, just a calm, familiar spot for the dog and a full-service groom from a trusted local pro. Appointments are set up by text or call, in English or Spanish, for dogs of every size and coat type.
