The name, and the window

Blackwater stays open

In 2019, the shop's front window on Newport Ave was smashed, and then damaged a second time not long after. The storefront got repaired and the doors stayed open. That is the shop in one photo: a real, working neighborhood parlor that keeps tattooing through the ordinary wear of running a business in Ocean Beach, not a polished brand exercise.

  • Storefront repaired and reopened after 2019 vandalism
  • Same Newport Ave address, still doing walk-in work
  • Roots that trace back to the 1996 Chula Vista original

Owner Mike Kellerman

Blackwater Tattoo is a real, working neighborhood shop, not a chain or a franchise. The Ocean Beach location traces its roots to 1996, when owner Mike Kellerman opened the original shop at 323 Broadway in Chula Vista. Walk in, sit down, and get real work from people who have earned their reputation the old-fashioned way, one client at a time.
