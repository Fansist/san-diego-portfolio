What it’s good for

Herbalremediesguide

Six common concerns, the herbs we reach for first, and what’s on the bench right now. Not medical advice — just what we’ve made for 8 years.

Sleep

Lavender · Chamomile · Valerian

Try: Chamomile Tea Blend + Lavender Cold-Process Soap

Steep the chamomile 8 minutes, wash with the soap before bed. Lights out.

Stress

Ashwagandha · Lemon Balm · Rose

Try: Cedar & Sage Tincture (5 drops under tongue)

Adaptogens take 2 weeks to peak. Take daily, not as-needed.

Skin

Calendula · Comfrey · Plantain

Try: Calendula Healing Salve

Patch-test first. Great on dry patches, minor scrapes, garden hands.

Immunity

Echinacea · Elderberry · Ginger

Try: Echinacea tincture (request custom batch)

Start at first sign of a tickle. 8-week maceration, made to order.

Digestion

Ginger · Fennel · Peppermint

Try: House ginger-fennel tea (loose-leaf)

After heavy meals. Steep covered 10 minutes so the oils don't escape.

Muscle

Arnica · Eucalyptus · Camphor

Try: Eucalyptus Chest Balm

Rub on sore spots or chest. Don't use on broken skin.
