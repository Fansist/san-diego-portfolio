The atelier journey

Bespoke, end to end

Four fittings, three weeks, one garment built around your body. No shortcuts, no machine-only construction, no surprises. This is the journey every bespoke commission takes.

I

Measure

Thirty points taken by hand — collar to cuff, nape to hem. We keep your card on file so future orders need only a quick confirm.

next
II

Cut & Baste

Pattern drafted from your measurements, fabric cut single-layer, then baste-stitched into a fitting shell. No machine seams yet.

next
III

Fit & Adjust

You try the basted shell. We re-pin, re-mark, and rebalance until the silhouette sits clean. Two fittings are standard; a third if the cloth demands it.

next
IV

Finish & Deliver

Single-needle lockstitch construction, hand-set collar, hand-finished buttonholes. Pressed, bagged, and delivered — usually three weeks from measure.
