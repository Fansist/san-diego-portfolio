How A Project Runs

From thirsty lawn to native habitat

This is the same sequence behind every turf-removal project, from a small front bed to a full yard conversion.

01

Consultation

An on-site visit to read the sun, soil, and slope, and talk through what the space could become.

02

Turf Removal

Thirsty lawn comes out, clearing the way for a planting plan built around this specific yard.

03

Native & Low-Water Planting

California natives and drought-tolerant species go in, chosen for habitat, food, and long-term water savings.

04

Irrigation & Ongoing Care

Irrigation adjusted for the new plan, then seasonal maintenance and pruning keep the garden thriving.

About the Studio

Design that works with San Diego’s climate, not against it

Gardens for the Future is a boutique, eco-conscious landscape design studio built around California native plants, low-water gardens, and permaculture practice. Every project centers native and drought-tolerant plantings, from full turf-removal conversions to edible garden beds and pollinator habitat. The approach stays intentionally small and expert rather than scaling into a large commercial landscaping outfit.
