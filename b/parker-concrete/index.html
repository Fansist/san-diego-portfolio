How It Works

How A Pour Happens

Six steps between a bare lot and a finished slab.

01

Site Prep & Excavation

The old surface is removed and the sub-grade graded and compacted so the new slab has a stable base.

02

Forming & Grading

Wood or metal forms set the shape, slope, and thickness of the pour, from a driveway edge to a curved patio.

03

Rebar & Base

Reinforcement and base material go down before a single truck of ready-mix arrives.

04

The Pour

Concrete is poured, screeded level, and floated smooth while it is still workable.

05

Finish & Stamp

Stamped patterns, exposed aggregate, or a broom finish get worked into the surface before it sets.

06

Cure & Seal

The slab cures, control joints are cut to manage cracking, and a sealer protects the finish long term.
