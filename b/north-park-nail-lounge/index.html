Our story

Familyowned,NorthParkproud

North Park Nail Lounge is a family-owned nail salon offering manicures, pedicures, waxing, and eyelash extensions, with a complimentary cocktail while you're pampered. Technicians train monthly on the newest trends and techniques, with close attention to clean, high-quality detail. The lounge keeps a standing birthday-week tradition: 15% off for you and your friends.
