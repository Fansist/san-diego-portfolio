The broth

Eighteen hours of low fire.

A 24-seat ramen shop on Convoy simmering pork bones for 18 hours. Noodles are made in-house daily; the spicy miso has a cult following that queues before open.

Bones prep · Simmer · Skim · Finish

The eighteen hours, broken down.

Four steps, no shortcuts. We start at 11 PM the night before service. Read it once — you'll taste the difference.

01

Bones Prep

11:00 PMCold

Blanch pork femurs and trotters for 30 minutes. Rinse, scrub, return to a clean pot. Cold water to cover by two inches.

02

Simmer

12:00 AM212°F

Rolling boil for the first hour, then drop to a hard simmer. Bones release collagen, marrow, calcium into the water.

03

Skim

Every 90 min205°F

Skim scum, foam, and excess fat. Repeat six times across the night. The broth turns from grey to ivory, then milky-white.

04

Finish

5:00 PM180°F

Strain through cheesecloth. Hold at 180°F for service. Ladle over tare, noodles, ajitama, scallion. Service begins.
