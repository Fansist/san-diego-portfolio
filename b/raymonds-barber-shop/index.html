Our Story

A Neighborhood Shop, The Old Fashioned Way

Raymond's Barber Shop has been cutting hair in North Park since 1978, now run by the third generation. Every haircut is finished the traditional way, with hot lather and a straight-razor cleanup on the neck and sideburns. The shop is family-friendly, kids and seniors sit in the same chairs, and a second location now serves Santee.

Two locations serve San Diego County: the original North Park shop on University Ave, and a second chair in Santee at 225 Town Center Pkwy.
