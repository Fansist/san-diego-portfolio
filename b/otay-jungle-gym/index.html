Novice To Pro

The Same Roof, Every Level

Otay Jungle Gym trains novice through professional athletes. This is the general path most members follow.

01

New To Training

Walk in with zero combat sports or gym background. Coaches build the fundamentals of stance, footwork, and basic strikes from the ground up.

02

Building Fundamentals

Technique rounds and strength and conditioning work layer on top of the basics, whether the goal is fitness or fight preparation.

03

Sparring & Conditioning

Members ready for live rounds move into sparring, supported by the weight room and turf room for the conditioning work behind it.

04

Competing & Pro Prep

The same facility supports athletes preparing for actual competition, from first amateur bout to professional camp.

About The Gym

A Training Warehouse In East Otay Mesa

Otay Jungle Gym is a multi-service training facility on the east side of Otay Mesa, housing Muay Thai, boxing, strength and conditioning, bootcamp, kettlebell, and TRX suspension training, plus sport performance programs, all under one roof. The facility trains novice through professional athletes and includes a large traditional weight room and a dedicated turf room for conditioning work. Long-time members describe the coaching staff as welcoming, whether training for general fitness or preparing to compete.

No polish, no boutique gloss. Just a full slate of combat sports and strength programs run out of one east Otay Mesa building.
