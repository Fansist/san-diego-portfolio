Where the name comes from

Four Fields, One Family

The name means “the four fields,” a nod to the small cornfields the family farmed before opening a counter in Barrio Logan in 1933. Ninety years later the same family still runs the kitchen, four generations deep, still rolling flour tortillas fresh every morning the same way they always have.

The original counter stood on Logan Avenue for decades. After a short closure, the family reopened on May 12, 2026 at Mercado del Barrio, a few blocks away, with the same short menu and the same wooden benches.

The family

Ninety Years, Same Hands

Las Cuatro Milpas has fed Barrio Logan since 1933, one of San Diego's oldest restaurants and still run by the same family four generations later. The menu has barely changed: handmade flour tortillas rolled fresh every morning, rolled tacos, tamales, and rice and beans swimming in savory broth, served on wooden benches with food passed straight over the counter. After a short closure, the family reopened on May 12, 2026 at Mercado del Barrio, a few blocks from the original Logan Avenue counter.
