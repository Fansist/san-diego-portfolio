Ray Castillo

Founder · Skin Fades & Pompadours

Ray's pompadours are why half of La Mesa's dads drive past two closer shops to sit in his chair. Skin fades, tapered crowns, the kind of part that holds through a Padres game. Trained in LA, came home in 2002, bought the chair he apprenticed in.

22 years behind the chair

“Plays upright bass in a surf-rock band on Sundays — says the steady hand for both is the same muscle.”

DR

Domingo 'Mingo' Reyes

Hot Towel Shaves & Beard Sculpting

Trained in Tijuana, never quite left the old-school hot-towel rhythm behind. Mingo will not be hurried — book 30 minutes for a shave, longer if you want the full works.

18 years behind the chair

“Once shaved a groom mid-air in a hot air balloon over Temecula. Long story, clean line.”

Teresa Nguyen

Kids Cuts & Line-ups

Speaks Vietnamese, Spanish, and a toddler dialect nobody else at the shop has cracked. The chair by the window is hers — stickers, a cumbia playlist, and first cuts that don't end in tears.

11 years behind the chair
