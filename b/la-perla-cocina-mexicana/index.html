Our story

Counter-service, no shortcuts

A San Diego mini-chain of counter-service taco shops with locations across Pacific Beach, Point Loma, and the Convoy District. Known for hefty California burritos, the signature OaxaCalifornia burrito, carne asada plates, and classic SoCal taco shop fare from breakfast through dinner. Frequently name checked on lists of San Diego's best hole in the wall Mexican food.
