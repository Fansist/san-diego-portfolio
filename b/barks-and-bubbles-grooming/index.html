Meet the groomers

The hands behind the bubbles

Three humans, one mission: send your pup home wiggling. Each one came to grooming from a different corner — vet tech, big-dog wrangler, cat person — and that mix is what makes the room work.

Maya R.

Eight years on the table. Started as a vet tech, switched to grooming because she missed the haircut part. Her scissor work is precise enough that you can see the breed standard in it.

Best with: doodles, poodles, anything scissored

Tomas P.

Came up grooming in North County, joined us in 2022. Big-dog guy — Berners, Newfies, anything that fills the doorway. De-shed blitzes are his signature.

Spirit pet: a Bernese named Juneau

Avery K.

Cat person, senior whisperer, spa-day specialist. Will sit on the floor with your nervous fourteen-year-old for as long as it takes.

Cats welcome · senior-safe
