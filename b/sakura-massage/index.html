Our story

Busyonpurpose

Sakura Massage is a busy, multi-therapist spa on Convoy Street in Kearny Mesa, open every day of the week from 10am to 10pm with walk-ins always welcome. The menu runs from Swedish and deep tissue to Thai, hot stone, shiatsu, and four hands sessions, with a complimentary CBD oil upgrade offered on couples massages. Close to 250 Yelp reviewers point to friendly, professional therapists, all without ever needing a reservation.
