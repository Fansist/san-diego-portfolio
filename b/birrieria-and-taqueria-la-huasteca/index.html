How it's made

From Potosi to the comal

Every plate follows the same five steps, unchanged since opening day.

  1. Step 1

    Potosino Family Recipes

    Recipes carried from the Potosi region of Mexico, the starting point for every pot.

  2. Step 2

    Slow-Cooked Birria

    Birria de res and birria de borrego, slow-cooked until fall-apart tender in a rich consome.

  3. Step 3

    Tortillas Pressed by Hand

    Corn tortillas pressed by hand on site every single day, no shortcuts.

  4. Step 4

    Griddled to Order

    Quesabirria tacos griddled and cheese-crisped, served with a cup of consome for dipping.

  5. Step 5

    Table, Takeout, or Truck

    Dine-in with outdoor seating, takeout at the counter, or delivery to your door.

Our story

Pacific Beach's first birrieria

Birrieria and Taqueria La Huasteca opened in December 2021 as Pacific Beach's first dedicated birrieria, built on Potosino family recipes carried to the coast. Birria de res and birria de borrego are slow-cooked until fall-apart tender, and tortillas are pressed by hand on site every day. Beyond the birria that built the name, the kitchen also serves quesabirria tacos, birria ramen, pizza birria, chilaquiles, menudo, and a chicken mole rooted in Potosino tradition.

Good to know

Frequently asked questions

Birria is a slow-cooked stew, traditionally made with goat, though beef and lamb are common today. We serve both birria de res (beef) and birria de borrego (lamb), prepared from family recipes carried from the Potosi region of Mexico.
