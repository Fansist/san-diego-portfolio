The plant care library

Six things every
aroid parent should know.

Light needs

Bright indirect keeps aroids happiest. East windows are gold; a few feet back from a south-facing window works too. Brown crisp edges mean too much sun.

Watering cadence

Top two inches dry, then drench. Aroids hate wet feet — a chunky aroid mix drains in seconds and roots stay oxygenated.

Humidity

Aim for 60% and above. Pebble trays, grouping plants, or a small humidifier all work. Crispy new-leaf edges are a humidity tell.

Repotting signs

Roots circling the pot, water running straight through, or growth stalled for a season — three signals it's time to size up.

Pest ID

Fine webbing under leaves = spider mites. Cotton tufts in crevices = mealybugs. Sticky residue = scale. Catch early, wipe with neem weekly.

Dormancy

Winter slow-down is normal. Hold fertilizer, water half as often, and don't panic over a yellow leaf or two. Spring will wake them.
