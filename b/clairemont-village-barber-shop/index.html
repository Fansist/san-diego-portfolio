The Shop

Same Chairs, Same Crew

Clairemont Village Barber Shop has been cutting hair in the Clairemont Village shopping center for roughly 55 years, a true walk-in neighborhood shop where regulars keep coming back for decades. The focus has never changed: traditional men's and boys' haircuts, tapers, fades, and clipper work at fair prices. Open seven days a week, it is one of the last honest, no-appointment-needed barbershops left in the neighborhood.

A true walk-in, community shop, plenty of our regulars have been coming back for decades.

Family Chairs

Bring The Whole Crew

Boys' haircuts sit right alongside men's cuts here, patient and friendly for younger clients in the same chairs their fathers and grandfathers used. Generations of Clairemont families have trusted this shop for a simple, honest haircut.
