Our story

A San Felipe family recipe, served since 2000

Mariscos German is a family run mariscos operation from San Felipe, Baja California, established in 2000 and credited as the first seafood lonchera in San Diego. The truck is known for crispy fish and shrimp tacos, marlin tacos, ceviche, clams, oysters, pata de mula, and aguachiles, plus a complimentary cup of hot seafood consome handed to every customer while they wait. It has become a cult favorite among San Diego taco truck fans, written up by food blogs like Kirbie's Cravings.
