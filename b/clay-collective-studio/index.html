Meet the makers

Member spotlight

MR

Maya R.

Member since 2021

Came to a Saturday beginner class having never touched clay. Three years later she throws dinner plates thin enough to see light through, and refills the floating-blue glaze bucket most Fridays herself.

— Speckled mug, floating blue

DT

Diego T.

Member since 2020

Spent a decade running production ceramics in LA before moving down. Soda-fire is his thing — tall vessels, shino glaze, raw rims left unglazed on purpose. Fires his own loads, stays late Wednesdays.

— Tall vase, shino + ash

PS

Priya S.

Member since 2022

Architect by day. Carves herringbone into leather-hard bowls and swears the trick is waiting two days longer than feels right.

— Herringbone bowl, butter cream

WK

Wren K.

Member since 2019

Studio's longest-tenured keyholder. Forages twigs from Balboa Park for pitcher handles — no two ever match, and that's the point.

— Twig-handled pitcher

Inside the studio

THE
FLOOR

The wheel room

12 wheels, 3 kilns, glaze bar

Featured

Wheel row

12 Brent CXC wheels, each with its own splash pan and stool. Mix of left- and right-handed setups.

Glaze bar

30+ house-mixed glazes, refilled every Friday afternoon. Members can blend their own test tiles.

Kiln yard

Three Skutt electric kilns — bisque and cone-6 glaze firings weekly. Soda-fire load every third Saturday.
