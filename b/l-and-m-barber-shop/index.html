Our Shop

A Clairemont Fixture For Sharp, Honest Cuts

L&M Barber Shop is a local Clairemont barbershop known for skin fades, beard shaves with a razor, and carved hair designs. It is the kind of shop where you drop in, get in the chair, and walk out looking right, no appointment required. Pricing stays simple and honest at $25 for a haircut and $15 for a beard trim, and the shop takes cash, cards, and Venmo.
