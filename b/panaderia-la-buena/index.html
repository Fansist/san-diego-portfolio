Our story

Cozy, traditional, and made the home-style way

Panaderia La Buena is a family-owned neighborhood panaderia on Imperial Avenue, baking traditional Mexican bread and pastries the way panaderias have always made them. The recipes follow home-style Villanueva, Zacatecas traditions, favoring authenticity over trends and warmth over flash, from fresh conchas and bolillos to pan dulce trays and custom cakes made to order.

  • Family-owned neighborhood panaderia
  • Home-style Villanueva, Zacatecas recipes
  • Custom cakes made to order
  • Open seven days a week
