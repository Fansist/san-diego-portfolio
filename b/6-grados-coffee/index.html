The wall, and the skull cup

Out front, a colorful mural of a masked figure holding a coffee cup marked with the shop's own “6” logo turns the exterior wall into a landmark regulars point out to friends. Inside, every cup carries the shop's “Death by Decaf” skull sticker, a small, playful signature on an otherwise traditional Mexican-inspired menu.

Our story

6 Grados Coffee is a quaint, artsy neighborhood coffee shop tucked into Paradise Hills, the kind of place regulars call a hidden gem.

The counter runs on Mexican-inspired espresso drinks and traditional coffee, from classic Café de Olla to creative lattes like Purple Rain and Bourbon Caramel, alongside pastries baked fresh every day. Ample seating, free WiFi, easy parking and an outdoor patio keep it feeling like a living room with excellent coffee.

“A hidden gem in National City.”
