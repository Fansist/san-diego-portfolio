Our Dojang

Small school. Long legacy.

Ko's Taekwondo is a neighborhood Korean martial arts dojang in Egger Highlands / San Ysidro teaching traditional Taekwondo to kids and adults. Originally opened as Lee's Taekwondo, the school was passed on to Master Ko in 2013, carrying forward a long-running local legacy of discipline-focused instruction. Classes stay close to the fundamentals: no gimmicks, just steady progress on the mat toward self-defense skill, focus, and confidence.

Families who train here point to the same thing: a structured class setting that builds self-defense skill, discipline, and patience in kids, taught without gimmicks and without a sales pitch.
